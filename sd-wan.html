Secure, High-Performance Connectivity for the Distributed Edge

Security-First Branch Transformation

Most SD-WAN solutions treat security as an afterthought. At Edgemonde, we believe the network is only as good as the protection it provides.

Palo Alto Networks SD-WAN is built natively into the industry-leading Next-Generation Firewall. It allows you to replace legacy, unsecure branch hardware with a single device that delivers top-tier performance and uncompromised security.

Core Benefits

🛡️

Unified Security & Networking

Stop managing two different stacks. By integrating SD-WAN into your firewall, you eliminate "security gaps" and simplify your branch footprint.

🚀

Superior Application Experience

Intelligent traffic steering ensures your most important tools—like Zoom, Teams, or Salesforce—always take the fastest path, even if a primary link fails.

💰

Drastic ROI Improvement

Leverage standard internet circuits instead of expensive MPLS without sacrificing performance. Organizations often see up to a 90% reduction in downtime.

⚡

Zero-Touch Provisioning

Ship a firewall to a new location, plug it in, and let the centralized controller handle the rest. New branches can be online and secure in minutes.
