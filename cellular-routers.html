Traditional wired connections are a single point of failure. Edgemonde offers Ericsson Cradlepoint solutions to provide enterprise-grade 5G and LTE connectivity that acts as more than just a backup.

The Velocity of Wireless WAN

Whether you are connecting a fleet of vehicles, a remote construction site, or a high-traffic retail branch, our Wireless WAN (WWAN) solutions deliver the speed of fiber with the flexibility of cellular.

Zero-Touch Scalability

Deep Cellular Intelligence

Zero Trust Architecture
