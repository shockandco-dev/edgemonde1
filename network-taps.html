NETWORK CRITICAL | TAPs

100% Visibility, Zero Packet Loss

Hardware-based data access ensuring your security and monitoring tools see every single bit of traffic without compromise.

Request a Technical Demo

Hardware-Based Data Access

Unlike SPAN/Mirror ports—which are prone to packet drops and timing issues—Network Critical TAPs provide a permanent, non-intrusive window into your infrastructure.

Engineered for Reliability

Comprehensive range of TAPs to suit any network architecture.

Passive Fiber TAPs

Power-independent units for high-speed fiber links. No configuration, no point of failure.

Active Copper TAPs

Fail-safe hardware for 10/100/1000Base-T networks that ensures link continuity during power loss.

Aggregating TAPs

Reduce tool sprawl by combining multiple traffic streams into a single monitoring output.

Regeneration TAPs

Replicate a single link to multiple output ports for simultaneous inspection by IDS and APM tools.

The Edgemond Advantage

Enterprise-grade benefits for your monitoring infrastructure.

Lossless Data Capture

Secure the full payload. TAPs capture physical layer errors and malformed packets that switches discard.

Architectural Integrity

TAPs have no IP or MAC address. They are invisible to the network and cannot be scanned or compromised.

Zero Network Latency

Maintain line-rate performance. TAPs do not process traffic; they copy it, ensuring zero impact on speed.

Critical Use Cases

Unfiltered Forensics

Provide an unaltered, time-stamped copy of traffic for incident response and breach post-mortems.

Continuous Compliance

Meet regulatory requirements (HIPAA, PCI-DSS) by ensuring all sensitive data is captured for auditing.

Intrusion Detection (IDS)

Feed clean, unsampled data to your security stack to prevent threats from slipping through bottlenecks.
