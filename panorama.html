Panorama™ Network Security Management

Centralized Control for the Modern Security Fleet

The Hub of Your Security Operations

Managing a distributed network shouldn't mean managing a hundred different interfaces. Panorama™ offers a single, streamlined point of control for your entire Palo Alto Networks infrastructure.

Whether your firewalls are physical appliances on-premise, virtual machines in the cloud, or containers in a microservices environment, Panorama ensures your security posture is consistent, visible, and under your command.

Core Benefits

🎯

Zero-Gap Policy Management

Create a single "Golden Rule" and deploy it to every firewall in your fleet instantly. No more manual, one-off configurations that lead to human error.

👁️

Total Network Visibility

Don't just look at logs—see the story. Panorama provides a graphical, interactive view of all traffic, allowing you to spot anomalies and threats in real-time.

📈

Enterprise-Grade Scalability

Built to grow with you. Manage up to 5000 firewalls from a single management pair, ensuring your infrastructure is never limited by its controller.

🤖

Automated Intelligence

Let the system do the heavy lifting. The automated correlation engine highlights high-priority risks, allowing your team to focus on resolution rather than data mining.
